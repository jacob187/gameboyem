#   expect: pass | fail      kind: blargg | mooneye | acid2 | smoke | play-snake | play-breakout

# the actual outcome differs from `expect` in either direction, so flipping a
# line from `fail` to `pass` is how a phase records progress, and regressions
# are caught. Paths may contain spaces (path is the rest of the line).
